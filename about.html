About · King’s Lynn

Eatwell Cafe
at 120
High St.

A proper sit-down cafe with a long menu, generous portions and the kind of opening hours that suit how people actually live. Walk-ins, families, takeaways, the lot.

A no-fuss family cafe right on the High Street — the kind of place where the breakfast lasts all day and the roast comes with proper gravy.

Eatwell is a working High Street cafe in the centre of King’s Lynn. We open at eight in the morning every day except Sunday, and we’re still cooking right through lunch, into the afternoon, and through to closing. Walk in for a full English at half nine, a jacket potato at one, a pasta dish at three, or a slice of cake and a coffee any time in between.

The menu runs broad on purpose. There are breakfasts and brunches, sandwiches and jackets, burgers and pasta, salads, omelettes, chicken dishes, a kids’ section, vegetarian and vegan plates, gluten-free options, Sunday roasts, daily specials, and proper desserts. Whatever you’re after, there’s something on it for you.

Inside there are around twenty tables, and out front under the red awning there’s more seating for when the weather turns out nice. We do sit-in, takeaway and delivery. It’s wheelchair accessible. Kids are welcome.

That’s the long and short of it. Come in, sit down, eat well.
