No. 01 · What we do

Three things, done properly.

Breakfast
all day.

Full English, eggs done your way, omelettes, brunch plates. Served from the moment we open until the last order before close — not just until 11.

Lunch &
dinner.

Burgers, jackets, roasts, pasta, sandwiches and specials. Generous portions, the way a proper cafe should be.

Sweet
treats.

Cakes, desserts and a proper drinks list. Stop in mid-afternoon for a slice and a cuppa, or finish off after a roast.
